# Population of the municipalities of Mexico, 1990-2040 (CONAPO)

Reconstruction (1990-2020) and projections (2021-2040) of the mid-year
population of the 2,475 municipalities of Mexico by sex and five-year
age group, published by the National Population Council (CONAPO).

## Usage

``` r
conapop
```

## Format

A tibble with 252,450 rows (municipality x sex x year) and 24 columns:

- cvegeo:

  five-digit municipality code of INEGI.

- nom_ent:

  name of the state.

- nom_mun:

  name of the municipality.

- sexo:

  sex: \`"HOMBRES"\` (men) or \`"MUJERES"\` (women).

- ano:

  year, 1990 to 2040.

- pob_00_04:

  population aged 0 to 4 years.

- pob_05_09:

  population aged 5 to 9 years.

- pob_10_14:

  population aged 10 to 14 years.

- pob_15_19:

  population aged 15 to 19 years.

- pob_20_24:

  population aged 20 to 24 years.

- pob_25_29:

  population aged 25 to 29 years.

- pob_30_34:

  population aged 30 to 34 years.

- pob_35_39:

  population aged 35 to 39 years.

- pob_40_44:

  population aged 40 to 44 years.

- pob_45_49:

  population aged 45 to 49 years.

- pob_50_54:

  population aged 50 to 54 years.

- pob_55_59:

  population aged 55 to 59 years.

- pob_60_64:

  population aged 60 to 64 years.

- pob_65_69:

  population aged 65 to 69 years.

- pob_70_74:

  population aged 70 to 74 years.

- pob_75_79:

  population aged 75 to 79 years.

- pob_80_84:

  population aged 80 to 84 years.

- pob_85_mm:

  population aged 85 years and over.

- pob_total:

  total population.
